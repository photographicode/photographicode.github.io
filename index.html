Questions owners ask

I already use Petpooja. Do I need to change?

No. Keep Petpooja for billing. We add the guest side: menu, table orders, Google page and video. Your staff can enter QR orders into billing as usual.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They point the phone camera at the QR and the menu opens in the browser in about a second.

What about older guests who don't use the phone?

Waiters still take orders the normal way. QR ordering is an extra lane, not a replacement.

Where does the payment money go?

Straight to your own UPI ID or Razorpay account. We never hold your money and take no commission.

Can guests order from home using the QR?

Each table's QR has a secret code, and staff see every order before cooking. You can pause phone ordering with one switch.

Can the menu be in Malayalam?

Yes. Dish names and descriptions can be in Malayalam, English, or both.

How long does setup take?

Send a photo of your menu on WhatsApp. We type it in, add your logo and colours, and bring QR cards. Usually live in 1–2 days.

What if the internet goes down?

Guests use their own mobile data, so the menu keeps working. Staff can always take orders by hand.
