Questions owners ask

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They point the phone camera at the QR and the menu opens in the browser in about a second.

What about older guests who don't want to use the phone?

Waiters can still take orders the normal way. QR ordering is an extra lane, not a replacement. Many restaurants keep one printed menu per table.

Where does the payment money go?

Straight to your own UPI ID or your own Razorpay account. Photographicode never holds your money and takes no commission.

Can guests order from home using the QR?

Each table's QR has a secret code, and staff see every order before cooking. You can also pause phone ordering any time with one switch.

Can I write the menu in Malayalam?

Yes. Dish names and descriptions can be in Malayalam, English, or both.

How long does setup take?

Send us a photo of your current menu on WhatsApp. We type it in, add your logo and colours, and deliver QR cards. Usually live in 1–2 days.

What if the internet goes down?

Guests use their own mobile data, so the menu keeps working. Your staff can always take orders by hand as usual.
